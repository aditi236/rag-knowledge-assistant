# Northwind Consulting: Travel and Expenses Policy

*Synthetic sample document for demonstrating the RAG pipeline. Northwind Consulting is fictional.*

## Submitting expenses

All expenses must be submitted through the finance portal within 30 days of being incurred. Receipts are mandatory for any single expense above USD 25. Claims submitted after 60 days will not be reimbursed.

## Flights and accommodation

Flights under 6 hours must be booked in economy class. Flights of 6 hours or longer may be booked in premium economy. Business class requires written approval from a director and is only granted for flights over 10 hours.

Hotel stays are capped at USD 220 per night in tier-1 cities (London, New York, Singapore, Dubai) and USD 150 per night elsewhere. Bookings must be made through the approved travel portal.

## Meals and per diem

The daily meal allowance while travelling is USD 65. Alcohol is not reimbursable. Client entertainment is reimbursable up to USD 100 per person and requires the client's name and company on the receipt.

## Approvals

Expense claims up to USD 500 are approved by the line manager. Claims between USD 500 and USD 2,000 need director approval. Anything above USD 2,000 must be pre-approved by the finance director before the spend occurs.
