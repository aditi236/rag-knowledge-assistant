# Northwind Consulting: Leave Policy

*Synthetic sample document for demonstrating the RAG pipeline. Northwind Consulting is fictional.*

## Annual leave

Full-time employees receive 25 days of paid annual leave per calendar year, plus public holidays. Leave accrues monthly at 2.08 days per month. Employees may carry over a maximum of 5 unused days into the next year; any additional unused days expire on 31 March.

Annual leave requests of more than 5 consecutive working days must be submitted at least 4 weeks in advance and approved by the line manager. Requests of 5 days or fewer need two weeks' notice.

## Sick leave

Employees receive 10 days of paid sick leave per year. A medical certificate is required for any absence longer than 2 consecutive days. Sick leave does not carry over.

## Parental leave

The primary caregiver is entitled to 16 weeks of fully paid parental leave. The secondary caregiver is entitled to 4 weeks of fully paid leave. Parental leave can be taken in a single block or split into two blocks within the first 12 months after the birth or adoption.

## Bereavement leave

Employees receive 5 paid days of bereavement leave on the death of an immediate family member (spouse, child, parent, sibling) and 2 paid days for extended family.
